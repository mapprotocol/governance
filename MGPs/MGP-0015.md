## Background
Since MAP Protocol added cross-chain support for Arc in September 2026, cross-chain activity involving Arc through MAP Protocol has remained low. This meets the persistently low usage ground for suspension set out in the chain listing and suspension criteria approved by the community in October 2026 (Proposal to Establish Chain Listing and Suspension Criteria for MAP Protocol).

The MAP Protocol Foundation has therefore determined that continued support for Arc is not justified and submits this Chain Support Suspension Proposal to suspend cross-chain support for Arc. The community is invited to vote on the proposal. This proposal reflects MAP Protocol’s focus on maintaining connections that serve active cross-chain demand.

## Scope of the Proposal
This proposal asks the community to approve the suspension of cross-chain transfers and messages, for all assets, with Arc as the source or destination through MAP Protocol-operated services.

It applies only to MAP Protocol’s Arc connection. Support for other chains is outside its scope.

## User Notice
If approved, the suspension will take effect 7 days after the voting result is published. Pending the outcome of this vote, new transfers involving Arc will not be available.

Following the vote, the Foundation will publish the result, any follow-up arrangements, and the updated list of supported and suspended chains. The team will review any outstanding transactions or withdrawal and redemption requests associated with the connection and provide guidance where user action is required.

## Future Reinstatement
Arc may be reconsidered for support if cross-chain demand increases. Reinstatement will require a new Chain Listing Proposal, evaluation against the listing criteria, and community approval.
